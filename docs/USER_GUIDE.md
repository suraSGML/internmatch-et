# InternMatch ET — User Guide

## For Students

### Getting Started
1. Go to `internmatch.et` and click **Register**
2. Select **Student / Graduate** as your role
3. Enter your name, email, and a strong password
4. Check your email and click the verification link
5. Log in and complete the **onboarding** (select your university and year of study)

### Completing Your Profile
A complete profile gets you better match scores. Go to **My Profile** and fill in:
- Bio (short introduction)
- Skills (type a skill and press Enter; click a skill to cycle its level)
- University, department, year of study
- Availability (when you can start, how many weeks, on-site/remote preference)
- City

### Finding Internships
- Browse at **Internships** — search by keyword and filter by field, city, work mode, pay, duration, stipend range, or eligible study year
- Sort by newest, closing deadline, or stipend; expired opportunities are excluded from open search results
- Your personal match score appears on recommendations and applications; recommendations explain the strongest matching factors
- Click **Save** (bookmark icon) to save internships for later
- Save a filtered search with a name to receive in-app alerts when a newly published listing matches it; manage or pause alerts under **Saved Searches**
- Open an employer profile from a listing to review its verified status, company information, and other open internships
- Sign in as a student to report a listing that appears misleading or inaccurate

### Applying
1. Open an internship and click **Apply Now**
2. Write a cover note (optional but recommended)
3. Answer any screening questions
4. Click **Submit Application**
- You can track all applications under **Applications**
- Each application shows a full timeline of status changes
- Application cards show the next step; interviews and saved listings with deadlines approaching receive in-app reminders

### After You Apply
- Check **Interviews** for scheduled interview dates — download `.ics` to add to your calendar
- Check **Placement** for pending offers — click **Accept** or **Decline**
- Once placed, go to **Logbook** to write weekly entries
- Check **Certificates** once the internship is complete

### CV Builder
Go to **My CV**, choose a template (Classic or Modern), and click **Download CV (PDF)**.
Your CV is generated automatically from your profile data.

## For Companies

### Getting Started
1. Register with the **Company / Organization** role
2. Complete the onboarding (company name, industry, city)
3. Upload your registration documents under **Company Profile**
4. Wait for admin verification (usually 1 business day)

### Posting an Internship
1. Go to **Internships** → **Post Internship**
2. Fill in Details (title, description, location, duration, deadline)
3. Add Required Skills under the Requirements tab
4. Set pay, benefits, and university approval requirement under Settings
5. Click **Publish Internship** (or **Save Draft** to finish later)

### Managing Applicants
- Go to **Internships** → click an internship → **Applicants**
- Applicants are shown in a Kanban pipeline, ranked by match score
- Use the **Move to** dropdown to advance applicants through stages
- Click **Schedule** to set up an interview (sends the student a notification)
- Click **Send Offer** once you have selected your candidate

### Managing Interns
- Go to **Interns** to see all active and past placements
- Click an intern to view their weekly logbook entries
- Approve or request revision on each entry
- Submit mid-term and final evaluations under the Evaluations tab
- Issue a completion certificate after the final evaluation

## For University Coordinators

### Getting Started
University coordinators are added by the InternMatch ET admin team.
Contact support@internmatch.et with your university name and coordinator email addresses.

### Approving Placements
- Go to **Placements** — pending placements appear at the top
- Review the student, company, internship, and dates
- Click **Approve** or **Reject** (provide a reason if rejecting)

### Issuing Placement Letters
- Go to **Placement Letters**
- Approved placements without a letter appear in the "Pending Letter" section
- Click **Issue Letter** — a unique verification code (e.g. `IM-DEMO0001`) is generated
- Share the verification link with the company: `internmatch.et/verify/IM-DEMO0001`

### Monitoring Logbooks
- Go to **Logbooks** to see all recent submitted entries from your students
- Note: only supervisors can approve/reject entries; coordinators have read-only access

### Reports
- Go to **Reports** for placement rate, department breakdown, and top host companies
- Click **Export CSV** to download a full placement report

### Announcements
- Go to **Announcements** to post messages to all your students
- Optionally target a specific department
